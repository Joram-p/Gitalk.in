What a new website needs before launch

A professional website is more than a homepage. Check the essentials before sharing it with visitors.

Core pages

  • About: explain who runs the site and what visitors can expect.
  • Contact: provide a working contact method.
  • Privacy policy: accurately describe data collection and third-party services.
  • Terms and disclaimers: explain limitations and any affiliate relationships where relevant.

Mobile and accessibility

Test on a phone, check text contrast, make buttons easy to tap, add meaningful image descriptions and ensure navigation works without a mouse.

Content and search

Publish original, useful pages that answer real questions. Use clear titles, descriptive headings, accurate metadata and links between related pages. Avoid copied content and mass-produced pages with little value.

Security and functionality

Test every link and form. Use HTTPS where available. Do not collect personal data until the form, storage and privacy disclosures are ready. A static site cannot securely run a server-side database or authentication by itself.

Plan a sustainable revenue model

Potential options include clearly disclosed affiliate links, advertising after approval, or selling a genuinely useful digital product or service. Each requires traffic, trust and compliance with the relevant platform's rules; none guarantees earnings.
