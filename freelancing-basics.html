A beginner's checklist for starting freelancing

Freelancing is a business activity, not instant income. Begin with a clear service and small proof of your skills.

1. Choose one service

Examples include translation, short-form video editing, basic design, writing, data cleanup or website updates. Pick something you can practise and deliver consistently.

2. Create sample work

Make two or three original samples that show your process and quality. Clearly label practice projects; never claim a sample was paid client work if it was not.

3. Define the offer

Explain what is included, the delivery time, how many revisions are included and what information you need from the client.

4. Use trustworthy platforms

Check fees, payment protection, identity requirements and dispute processes. Keep conversations and payments on-platform when the platform requires it.

5. Watch for scams

  • Do not pay a “registration fee” to receive a job.
  • Be cautious of overpayments, fake cheques and requests to move money.
  • Never share one-time passwords or account passwords.
  • Agree on scope and payment terms before starting work.

Remember: Getting clients can take time, and income is not guaranteed. Keep improving your skills and track expenses and earnings carefully.
