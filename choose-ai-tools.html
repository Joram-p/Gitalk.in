How to choose AI tools without wasting money

Start with the task you need to complete—not with a subscription. A little comparison can save time and money.

1. Define the job

Write down one specific task: drafting, summarising, image creation, video editing, research or organising work. Tools that specialise in your task may be easier to evaluate.

2. Test the free option first

Check free-plan limits, export quality, watermarks, usage caps and whether your workflow is supported. Do not assume “free” means unlimited.

3. Check privacy and rights

Read how a service handles uploaded files, prompts and personal information. For commercial work, review the provider's terms for output rights and permitted uses.

4. Compare the full cost

Look at renewal prices, currency conversion, taxes, cancellation rules and the features locked behind paid tiers. Avoid paying for overlapping tools you rarely use.

5. Keep a simple scorecard

  • Does it solve the task reliably?
  • Is the output quality good enough?
  • Can you export or reuse the result?
  • Are privacy terms and costs acceptable?

Bottom line: Choose the smallest toolset that does the job well. AI tools can support work, but they do not guarantee income or business success.
