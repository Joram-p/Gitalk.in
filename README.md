## Before publishing
1. Open `js/products.js`.
2. Replace `GITALK_AFFILIATE_LINKS` with your exact affiliate URLs. For production, use one exact link per product rather than the demo rotating list.
3. Replace placeholder gallery/product visuals with your own files under `assets/`.
4. Connect the contact form to a secure backend/email service.
5. Connect live weather/news/FX APIs through a backend.
6. Connect real payment processing only through a secure backend and your chosen payment provider.
7. Add real authentication/database/storage for `admin.html`.
8. Connect a real AI API from the backend; never put an AI secret key in browser JavaScript.
9. Test all pages, forms and links on mobile and desktop.
10. Upload the frontend files to your hosting and configure HTTPS/DNS.

## Important
This ZIP is a working static frontend/demo. It does NOT process real payments, send real email, provide live news/weather/FX, or provide real AI by itself.
