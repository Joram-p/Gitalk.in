Speaking Practice Studio

नीचे दिए dialogues को ज़ोर से पढ़ें। पहले धीरे, फिर natural speed में बोलें।

1. Self Introduction

A: What is your name?

B: My name is Rahul.

A: Where do you live?

B: I live in Rajasthan.

A: What do you do?

B: I am learning English.

2. At the Market

A: How much is this?

B: It is one hundred rupees.

A: Can you give me a discount?

B: Yes, I can.

3. Asking for Help

A: Excuse me, can you help me?

B: Of course. What do you need?

A: I am looking for the bus station.

B: Go straight and turn left.

4. Daily Routine

I wake up early.

I brush my teeth.

I have breakfast.

I go to work.

I practice English every day.

Speaking challenge: आज 60 seconds तक अपने बारे में English में बोलें। Phone में recording करें और दोबारा सुनें।
